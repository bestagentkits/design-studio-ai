4. **Browser failure and cause.** Mobile scene checks found blank composited 3D pixels after returning from a hidden pane. A zero-sized WebGL drawing buffer reached `drawImage`, whose rejection could terminate the animation loop. DOM presence and layer-order assertions alone had missed this failure; the pixel check exposed it.
5. **Cause-aligned repair.** [SceneView](../../../src/app/scene-view.tsx) now ignores zero-sized measurements when resizing the renderer and invalidates rendering after a valid resize. The [scene compositor](../../../src/shared/scene-composition.ts) also skips zero-sized source buffers before drawing. The two guards protect hidden mobile panes while allowing rendering to resume when the canvas becomes visible.
